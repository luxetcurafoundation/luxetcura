Youth Service Microgrants

Small grants for young people with ideas to make a difference in their communities.

Lux et Cura Foundation is developing a microgrant program to help young people turn ideas for community service into action. Grants will support practical, youth-led projects that respond to real community needs and create opportunities for meaningful service, leadership, and connection.

Get Involved Contact Us

About the Program

We want to make it easier for young people to take a good idea and make it happen.

What we hope to support

Projects that address a community need through hands-on service, outreach, education, or other practical efforts.

Projects may be small. What matters is that they are thoughtful, achievable, and led by young people who want to contribute to their community.

What comes next

We are still developing the program. Additional information about eligibility, grant amounts, application deadlines, and the selection process will be posted as those details are finalized.

We expect to begin with a small number of grants and grow the program over time.

Have an Idea?

You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out.

Tell us about it

We are interested in hearing from young people who have an idea for a service project, community initiative, or other effort that could benefit others.

Stay in touch

For now, please contact us at info@luxetcura.org. We will share application information here as the microgrant program takes shape.
